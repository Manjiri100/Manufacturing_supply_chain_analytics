# Power BI Dashboard Wireframe

## 1 Executive Operations Overview
KPI cards: Procurement Late Rate, Inventory Value, Production Attainment, Shipment Late Rate, Quality Failure Rate. Add monthly trends and top operational risks.

## 2 Supplier & Procurement Performance
Supplier ranking, late rate, spend exposure, plant comparison and monthly procurement trend.

## 3 Inventory & Warehouse Risk
Inventory value, shortage rows, allocation rate, warehouse ranking and high-value shortage matrix.

## 4 Production & Plant Efficiency
Attainment, schedule variance, underproduction and plant risk ranking.

## 5 Logistics & Shipment Performance
Late rate, average delay, warehouse performance, customer risk and volume.

## 6 Quality & Machine Events
Failure rate, defect mix, machine downtime, severity and plant risk relationship.

## 7 Data Quality & Trust
Issue counts by domain, issue type, affected records and KPI trust indicators.
